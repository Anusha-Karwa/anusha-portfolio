The Decision Room

Don't read her judgment. Test it.

Real calls from your work become playable cards. The visitor picks what they would do, the card flips to show what you did and what happened, and a stamp lands. At the end they see how their instincts compare with yours. Interviews for AI PM roles test judgment, and this lets you show it before the interview.

Info arrives as
3D card flips, outcome stamps slamming down, a progress path filling, a final match score.
